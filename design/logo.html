Best Practices

Brand Guidelines

Comprehensive guidelines to maintain brand integrity, consistency, and impact across all customer touchpoints.

✓ Do's — Logo Use

  • Always maintain proper spacing and proportion when scaling
  • Use the full horizontal lockup for primary branding (websites, reports)
  • Use the icon mark for small applications (64px+)
  • Apply the logo with intentional white space around it
  • Use the light version on light backgrounds, dark version on dark backgrounds
  • Preserve the navy primary color for wordmark (do not change)
  • Keep the O ring and arrow unified in color and meaning
  • Scale SVG versions for crisp rendering at all sizes

✗ Don'ts — Logo Misuse

  • Don't resize without maintaining aspect ratio
  • Don't change the colors without strategic justification
  • Don't rotate, skew, or distort the logo
  • Don't add drop shadows, glows, or effects unless specified
  • Don't separate the O ring from the wordmark in primary applications
  • Don't place the logo on backgrounds with insufficient contrast
  • Don't use the logo at sizes smaller than 64px × 64px (icon) or 240px width (lockup)
  • Don't create outlines or stroke variations without approval

Application Contexts

  • Website Headers: Horizontal lockup, 300–400px width, positioned left-aligned in navbar
  • PDF Reports: Horizontal lockup at top-left, 280px width, with subtle accent line
  • Email Signatures: Icon mark, 120–128px, followed by "Opryn OS" in small text
  • WhatsApp Profile: Icon mark, 640 × 640px (auto-scaled to 64px in DP)
  • Social Media: Square icon mark (1:1 ratio), 512 × 512px minimum
  • Business Cards: Horizontal lockup at top, minimum 240px width for print clarity
  • Favicon: Icon mark, 32 × 32px (platform will auto-scale)

Color Application Rules

  • Navy (#0D1B2A) for wordmark on light backgrounds; cream (#f0ece4) on dark
  • Primary Blue (#1B6CA8) for O ring and accent elements on all backgrounds
  • Gradient (Light Blue #7ab8fb → Primary Blue #1B6CA8) for arrow and motion elements
  • Maintain at least 2:1 contrast ratio with background for accessibility
  • Never use single colors for gradient; always apply the diagonal 45° gradient for arrows

Spacing & Safe Area

  • Clear space (minimum 8px) around all sides of the logo
  • No other elements should intrude into the safe area
  • Spacing should increase with logo size (proportional to overall design)
  • In lockup format, maintain even spacing between icon and wordmark
Design Philosophy

Why This Design

The Opryn logo is intentionally designed to bridge two worlds: Fortune 500 operational systems and WhatsApp-native accessibility. Every element serves a purpose.

The O Ring: Operational Circle

The circular O symbolizes completeness, continuity, and the cyclical nature of business operations. It's solid yet refined—no sharp edges, no aggressive geometry. It feels trustworthy and grounded.

The Upward Arrow: Growth Momentum

The arrow inside the O points upward, signifying growth, optimization, and upward trajectory. It's not floating—it's anchored within the O, meaning growth is grounded in operational excellence, not hype.

The Gradient: Energy & Motion

The diagonal gradient from light to darker blue adds visual energy and suggests data flow, intelligence in motion. It modernizes the mark without making it feel trendy or temporary. It works at 64px and at 1000px.

Opryn Wordmark: Modern & Precise

The wordmark is set in Outfit (geometric sans-serif) with tight letter spacing (-0.8px), conveying modern precision and custom craftsmanship. The navy color grounds the design in trust and enterprise authority.

Dual Color Identity: Confidence & Approachability

Navy conveys Fortune 500 stability and financial institutions' trust. Blue conveys technology, innovation, and approachability. Together, they say: "We're serious about your data, but we're not intimidating."
